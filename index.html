Controls

WASDWalk (on the bike: throttle / steer / brake)
MouseLook around
Shift+WRun (you will get tired). While pushing the bike: your friend joins in; two people push about twice as fast as one
CCrouch / hide (hold)
XSwitch first-person / third-person camera
FFlashlight on / off. While you push the bike it switches the bike's headlight instead of the phone
EInteract · at any table: leave / pick up your phone and key
EPick up twigs · at a fire-pit: light it (2 twigs) or feed it
TBuild a fire where you stand (3 twigs). Warmth eases tiredness and fear
Shift+EPut an item back (key / phone / helmet)
Left click (while riding)Horn
E (while riding)Start / stop the engine
Shift+BNear the bike: hang the helmet on the rear holder by its chin strap, or take it back
GPush the bike by hand (rough ground) · B to ride it
BBike: get on and start it (needs the key) · switch off and get off
IInventory: phone, key, helmets, twigs, matches, plug, petrol... (also in the pause menu)
PPhone on / off (bottom-right: time, network, flashlight, settings, menu)
MOpen the map (shows where you are, which way you face, and where to go)
HStuck? Hops you a few metres ahead along the route
EscPause. In menus: press the highlighted letter (N New game, S Settings, M Main menu...), B to go back

No fighting. No inventory. Just two friends, and a night. The gold marker always shows where to go next.
